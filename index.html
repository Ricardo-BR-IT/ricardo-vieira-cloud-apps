Forge Cloud Productivity Suite — Documentation & Privacy Policy

Official documentation, support policy, and privacy statement for our Atlassian Forge Cloud applications:

  • Epic Rollup & Time Progress Bar Pro (for Jira Cloud)
  • Mermaid v11 Diagrams & LaTeX Math Studio Pro (for Confluence Cloud)

1. Privacy Policy & Data Security ("Runs on Atlassian")

Effective Date: November 2026

All of our applications are built 100% natively on the Atlassian Forge platform:

  • Zero External Egress: Your Jira issues, Confluence pages, user data, and diagrams are processed exclusively inside Atlassian's hosted Forge runtime and encrypted Forge Storage.
  • No Third-Party Servers: We do not transmit, sell, or store any customer data on external servers.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: All app storage resides within your Atlassian Cloud instance and is automatically purged by Atlassian upon app uninstallation.

2. Documentation: Epic Rollup & Time Progress Bar Pro

Zero Configuration Required: Once installed from the Atlassian Marketplace, open any Jira Epic or Parent Issue (in either Team-Managed / Next-Gen or Company-Managed projects) and click the Epic Rollup & Time Progress Pro button under the issue summary.

  • Automatically rolls up child issue Status Categories (To Do, In Progress, Done), Story Points, and Time Tracking (Logged vs. Original Estimate).
  • Highlights over-budget child issues in red (⚠️).
  • Click Copy Stakeholder Summary to copy a Markdown report table or Export CSV to download spreadsheet data.

3. Documentation: Mermaid v11 & LaTeX Math Studio Pro

Type /Mermaid v11 & LaTeX Math Studio Pro inside any Confluence Cloud page to insert the macro.

  • Supports full Mermaid v11 syntax (Flowcharts, Sequence Diagrams, ER Diagrams, State Diagrams) and KaTeX / LaTeX mathematical expressions.
  • Automatically adapts to Light and Dark Mode.
  • Includes 1-click starter templates and SVG export.
